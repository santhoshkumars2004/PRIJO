Who We Are

WE ARE PRIJO

Born from the streets of India, PRIJO is a fashion label for the bold, the creative, and the unapologetically different. We blend Indian craftsmanship with global streetwear aesthetics to create pieces that speak without saying a word.

Every stitch tells a story. Every piece is a canvas. We don't follow trends — we set them. From the vibrant lanes of Indian cities to the global fashion stage, PRIJO carries the spirit of fearless individuality.

OUR VALUES

✨

Quality First

We source the finest fabrics and work with skilled artisans to create garments that look premium and last longer. Every piece is inspected for perfection before it reaches you.

🎨

Culture Driven

Our designs draw inspiration from India's rich cultural tapestry — from street art to architecture, music to cinema. We celebrate the beautiful chaos of Indian urban life.

🌍

Sustainable Fashion

We believe in responsible fashion. Organic materials, ethical production, minimal waste packaging — because looking good should never cost the planet.

OUR JOURNEY

2020

PRIJO was born — a small idea sketched on a napkin in a Mumbai café. Just two friends with a dream to redefine Indian streetwear.

2021

First collection launched: "Street Roots." 500 pieces sold out in 48 hours. The community noticed.

2022

Expanded to women's wear. Opened our first pop-up store in Delhi's Hauz Khas Village. Featured in Vogue India.

2023

Launched sustainable packaging initiative. Collaborated with 10 Indian artists. Crossed 50,000 orders milestone.

2024

Introduced oversized and gender-fluid collections. Partnered with local weavers in Jaipur and Varanasi for exclusive fabrics.

2025

Season 01 drops — our most ambitious collection yet. International shipping launched. The story continues...
